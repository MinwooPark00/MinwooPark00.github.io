Symmetry-Aware Steering of Equivariant Diffusion Policies: Benefits and Limits
Minwoo Park*, Junwoo Chang*, Jongeun Choi, Roberto Horowitz
IFAC World Congress, 2026
arXiv / code

The diffusion process of an equivariant diffusion policy induces a group-invariant latent-noise MDP, so steering it with equivariant RL improves sample efficiency and stability, up to the limits set by symmetry breaking.

Partially Equivariant Reinforcement Learning in Symmetry-Breaking Environments
Junwoo Chang, Minwoo Park, Joohwan Seo, Roberto Horowitz, Jongmin Lee, Jongeun Choi
ICLR, 2026
project page / arXiv / OpenReview

Real environments are rarely symmetric everywhere, so a partially group-invariant MDP gates between group-invariant and standard Bellman backups, keeping the benefits of equivariance where symmetry holds without spreading errors from where it breaks.
